# Equity Research & Valuation Model of Avenue Supermarts (DMart)

## 🦅 Overview
Through this project, I have tried to build and present a comprehensive equity research and valuation analysis of Avenue Supermarts Ltd. (henceforth DMart), one of India's leading value chains.

The objective of the project was to evaluate the intrinsic value of DMart using fundamental analysis, financial statement forecasting, and discounted cash flow valuation techniques while assessing the company's long-term growth prospects.

## Project Objectives
Analyze DMart's business model and competitive positioning.
Evaluate historical financial performance.
Forecast future operating performance.
Estimate intrinsic value using Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) methodology.
Compare valuation with market pricing.
Assess key risks and investment considerations.

#### Relative Valuation
EV/EBITDA
Price-to-Earnings (P/E)

### Sensitivity Analysis
Valuation sensitivity was assessed across multiple assumptions for:
WACC
Terminal growth rate
Revenue growth

## Key Findings
DMart continues to demonstrate strong operating efficiency and capital allocation.
Store expansion remains a major growth driver.
Working capital management is significantly stronger than many retail peers.
Intrinsic value is highly sensitive to growth and discount rate assumptions.
